## Description (max 10,000 characters)

```
folio does everything you need with a PDF, entirely on your own computer.

Most free PDF tools are websites. You upload a contract, a payslip, a passport scan - to a server you know nothing about, run by a company whose business model you cannot see. folio never does that, because it cannot: there is no networking code in the app at all.

WHAT IT DOES

Organise
- Merge several PDFs into one, in the order you choose
- Split out a page range, burst into single pages, or cut every N pages
- Reorder and delete pages by dragging thumbnails
- Rotate pages

Optimise
- Compress with three strengths, from lossless repacking to aggressive image optimisation

Convert
- Turn images into a PDF, and export PDF pages as PNG or JPEG
- Make scanned documents searchable with built-in OCR

Security and privacy
- Password-protect with 256-bit or 128-bit AES, and set printing, copying and editing permissions
- Remove a password you know
- Redact properly: the text and images under your black boxes are deleted from the file, not merely covered up
- View, edit or completely strip document metadata

Everything else
- Watermark pages with your own text
- Batch mode applies any of the above to a folder full of files at once, and tells you exactly which ones worked

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

It is genuinely offline. Not "we don't sell your data" - there is no code in this application capable of making a network request. It works with the network cable unplugged, on an air-gapped machine, forever. The Store package declares no network capabilities at all, which you can verify yourself.

It is genuinely free. Every feature, for everyone, including businesses, with no user limit, no trial, no watermark on your output, no locked "pro" tier and no nagging. There is nothing to upgrade to.

It is genuinely open. folio is licensed under the GPL-3.0, so anyone can read the source and confirm all of the above rather than taking a marketing page's word for it.

Redaction actually redacts. Drawing a black rectangle over a name in most editors leaves the name sitting in the file, one "select all, copy" away. folio rewrites the page's content stream to delete the glyphs and images inside your selection, then draws the box.

WHAT IT WILL NEVER DO

No telemetry, not even the optional kind. No accounts. No cloud sync. No advertising. No paid tier. And no feature bloat - folio aims to do a dozen things flawlessly rather than fifty things adequately.

REQUIREMENTS

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, 64-bit.

OPEN SOURCE

Source code, issue tracker and the full licence: see the support link on this page.
```

## Notes on fields the copy above deliberately avoids

- **No superlatives that certification questions.** "The best PDF app" invites
  a 10.1 review; "runs entirely on your own computer" is a verifiable claim.
- **No comparisons by name.** Naming competitors in a Store listing is a common
  rejection reason.
- **"Free forever" appears once**, as a factual statement about licensing
  rather than a promotional banner.
